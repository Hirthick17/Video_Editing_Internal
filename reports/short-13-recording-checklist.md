## 2. Filming Setup Standards
- **Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) at 30.0 FPS.
- **Lighting:** Warm natural desk lamp or soft window light angled at 45 degrees to avoid screen reflection/glare.
- **Framing:** Medium close-up of phone screen resting on desk, or handheld over-the-shoulder POV.
- **Exposure:** Lock exposure on the phone screen so UI text remains sharp and legible.
- **Environment:** Clean desk surface (notebook, pen, laptop edge in background).

## 4. Intake Quality Gate Checklist
Before ingesting any new video clip into `video-editor/`:
- [ ] Strictly authentic live footage (NO synthetic diffusion or AI video).
- [ ] 1080×1920 vertical orientation.
- [ ] Constant frame rate (30 FPS preferred).
- [ ] No confidential personal tenant details (phone numbers, full bank account numbers) visible without blur.
- [ ] Ingested via `AssetGuardrailService` to generate SHA-256 and register in `asset_manifest.json`.
