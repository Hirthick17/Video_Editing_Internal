# 06 — Brand and Editing Rules

## 1. Current confirmed rules

- The channel is Hirthick's Vision.
- The content should communicate practical product/business value to a non-technical audience.
- The editing experience must remain owner-controlled and editable in ChatCut.
- Real recordings and approved stock footage are the visual foundation.
- No generated video clips.
- No generated SVG cards or SVG overlay layer.
- No generated images, illustrations, synthetic B-roll, or motion graphics.
- Do not replace authentic product demonstrations with fabricated product visuals.
- Do not invent brand treatments or silently change the creative direction.

## 2. Existing 12 November test choices (not automatically permanent brand rules)

- Test output: 1080 × 1920, vertical 9:16.
- Test frame rate: 30 FPS.
- Test duration: 30 seconds / 900 frames.
- Stock spreadsheet clip on V2.
- Real product/demo recording on V1.
- Separate audio track A1.

These are test settings from Short #13, not universal rules for every future video. Confirm per project where needed.

## 3. Creative approach

Use:
- authentic product screen recordings;
- real phone/tabletop footage recorded by the owner;
- approved stock footage with source/license details;
- restrained, readable native text/captions where approved;
- purposeful cuts and pacing;
- clear speech-first audio mixing.

Do not use:
- AI-generated video;
- generated SVG graphics or card sequences;
- synthetic product UI;
- decorative visual layers created without approval;
- unnecessary transitions, effects, or motion graphics.

## 4. Brand values still requiring owner confirmation

Do not guess:
- logo file and placement;
- font family;
- brand colors;
- caption position and style;
- music library/source;
- preferred transition family;
- maximum stock-footage proportion;
- standard ending/outro sequence;
- whether the test's dark green/WhatsApp palette is specific to TellOS or intended for the channel generally.

## 5. Audio rules

- Speech intelligibility takes priority over music.
- Use only music with a documented license/usage basis.
- Do not assume that a stock-video license also covers its audio.
- Avoid clipping, abrupt level changes, and music masking speech.
- Verify the final rendered audio, not only the timeline settings.
